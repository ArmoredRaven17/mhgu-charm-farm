Other MHGU Apps

Companion fan tools by the same author. Each opens in a new tab.

  • Quest Randomizer Rolls random quests when you can't decide what to hunt.
  • Collection Tracker Tracks which weapons, armor and Palico gear you own, and what it costs to finish them.
  • Equipment Box Mirrors the in-game box — 2000 hunter slots and 1000 Palico — so you can plan where things sit.
  • Bingo Builds a bingo card of hunting goals, with a shareable seed so a group can race the same board.
  • Hunting Log Keeps a running record of your hunts — what you were after, what you wore, who came along.
  • Zenny Gauntlet A scored challenge run — fail a hunt or get carted and you lose that weapon/style combo for the rest of the run.
  • Set Builder Assemble a full set by hand and see exactly which skills it activates.
  • Weapon Trees Browse every weapon upgrade tree, with full stats on any node you click.
  • Challenge Run A permadeath challenge run — start from a root weapon, lose it on any quest failure, clear every Key Quest.
  • Charm Farm you are here Beat charms out of a Brachydios, then meld three of a rarity into one fresh roll.

The roster

Every Brachydios below the Raging one is the same sprite wearing an ore's colours. Which ones can show up depends on your rank, matching the ranks that ore can actually be mined at in the game.

About / Attributions

An unofficial, non-commercial fan toy: grind a Brachydios for talismans, then feed three of a rarity into the Melding Pot for one fresh roll of that rarity. The charms it rolls are real — every skill and point value comes from the game's own charm tables, so nothing it produces is something MHGU couldn't give you.

Development: Built by the author with substantial help from Claude Code, Anthropic's AI coding tool. Most of the code, the data-extraction scripts and this page were written with AI assistance, reviewed and directed by the author.

Charm tables, ore names, prices and mining ranks: derived from Kiranico and the community MHGU database.

English item, skill and equipment names: MHGUDB-iOS (MIT) & gatheringhallstudios community database. The game files store names in Japanese only, so the English naming throughout this tool comes from their work.

Talisman icons: monsterhunterwiki.org — licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0; adaptations shared under the same licence.

Monster and ore icons: Monster Hunter Wiki (Fandom) — licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0; adaptations shared under the same licence. The ore-coloured Brachydios variants are CSS recolours of the base icon and are shared under that same licence.

Menu textures and font: carried over from the MHGU Collection Tracker.

Monster Hunter content is owned by Capcom. This tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by Capcom.
